      How progression works

      Double progression. Stay at the same weight until you can hit the top of the rep range on every set with clean form. Then add the smallest jump available and start again at the bottom of the range.

      Tempo notation. "3-1-1" means three seconds lowering, a one second pause, one second lifting. Slow lowering is where a large share of the growth stimulus lives — especially with lighter dumbbells.

      Reps in reserve. Most sets should finish with about two good reps left in the tank. Only the marked finishers go all the way to failure.

      Logging matters. Whatever you record here shapes the next session — loads, rep targets and exercise selection all adjust based on your last three workouts.
